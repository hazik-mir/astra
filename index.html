Find local businesses that may need a website

Astra helps web designers, freelancers and digital agencies find potential local clients. It searches public OpenStreetMap data for mapped businesses with a phone number but no website field. Results can be incomplete or outdated, and some businesses may have a website on social media or elsewhere, so verify each lead before calling.

Be respectful when contacting businesses, follow local calling and privacy rules, and stop if someone asks not to be contacted. Data coverage varies by area; no matching results does not mean no businesses exist.
